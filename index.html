Foster Starter Kit Giveaway: Terms and Conditions

Before I leave Australia in mid-November, I'm trying to help 4 more cats by getting 4 brand-new foster carers set up with supplies. These terms explain exactly how it works.

The short version

  1. You've never fostered an animal before, you're 18 or over, and you live in Australia.
  2. You get approved as a foster carer with a registered animal rescue, on or after 23 October 2026.
  3. You DM me a screenshot of your approval. The first 4 valid claims get up to $65 of foster supplies from Kmart, delivered to you.
  4. Winners are featured on my account by first name, rescue and city (no faces), with updates on their foster cat.

Full terms

01

Who runs this

This giveaway is run by me personally, as the owner of the account that posted the "Can I save 5 cats before I leave Australia?" video. It is not run, sponsored or endorsed by any animal rescue, Kmart, Instagram, Meta or TikTok. By taking part you release Instagram and TikTok from any responsibility for it.

02

Who can claim

To claim a kit you must:

  • be 18 or over and live in Australia, with an Australian delivery address;
  • never have been a foster carer for any animal, with any rescue, shelter or council, before this giveaway;
  • be approved as a foster carer by a registered animal rescue organisation in Australia (for example, one listed on PetRescue), with that approval given on or after 23 October 2026.

One kit per person and one per household.

03

How to claim

Send me a direct message on the account where you saw the video, with:

  • a screenshot of your foster approval (an email or message from the rescue) that shows the rescue's name and the date. You can blur anything else that's personal.
  • a line confirming you've never fostered before;
  • the key phrase, word for word. It's at the end of these terms.

DMs without the key phrase won't be counted.

I may ask a follow-up question, or check with the rescue, to confirm your approval.

04

First 4 valid claims

This is not a random draw. Kits go to the first 4 claims that meet these terms, in the order the complete DMs arrive. An incomplete claim holds its place in line only once it's complete. I'll update the video series as kits are claimed, and post when all 4 are gone.

05

Dates

Claims open on 23 October 2026 and close at 11:59pm AEDT on Sunday 15 November 2026, or earlier once all 4 kits have been claimed.

06

What you get

Up to $65 AUD of cat foster supplies, ordered by me from Kmart Australia and delivered to your address. The suggested kit is a carrier, litter box, litter scoop, food and water bowls, a scratcher and a blanket. If you already have some of these, we can swap items for others from Kmart, within the $65 limit.

  • The kit is supplies only. It can't be exchanged for cash or gift cards, and it can't be transferred.
  • If an item is out of stock, I'll swap it for something similar.
  • Delivery is handled by Kmart. I'm not responsible for delivery delays, damage in transit or product faults. Those go through Kmart's normal returns process.
07

Your fostering

Your foster arrangement is between you and your rescue. I'm not involved in approvals, matching cats, vet care or anything else about your placement. If fostering doesn't work out, please pass the supplies on to your rescue so they still help a cat.

08

Your details

I'll only use your full name, address and phone number to place your Kmart delivery, which means sharing them with Kmart. I won't share them with anyone else or post them, and I'll delete them from our messages once your order has arrived.

09

Featuring winners

So everyone can see the kits went to real foster carers, featuring winners is part of the giveaway. By claiming a kit, you agree that I can share publicly on my Instagram and TikTok (in posts, reels or stories):

  • your first name;
  • the rescue that approved you;
  • your city or town, and state.

I won't show your face unless you ask me to. After the giveaway closes, I'll check in now and then to ask for photos or stories about your foster cat to share. Sending them is optional but very welcome. If you'd like something taken down later, DM me and I'll remove it.

10

Fair play

I can refuse a claim that doesn't meet these terms, or that looks false or misleading. If something outside my control comes up, I may change or end the giveaway, and I'll say so publicly on the account. My decisions about claims are final.

11

The key phrase

If you've read this far, thank you. Put this exact sentence in your claim DM to show you've read and agree to these terms:

"I agree to the T&Cs, and Tictac deserves bum pats."
