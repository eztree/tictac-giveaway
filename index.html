Foster Starter Kit Giveaway: Terms and Conditions

Before I leave Australia in mid-November, I'm trying to help 4 more cats by getting 4 brand-new foster carers set up with supplies. These terms explain exactly how it works.

The short version

  1. You've never fostered an animal before, you're 18 or over, and you live in Australia.
  2. You apply to foster a cat with a registered animal rescue between 23 October and 1 November 2026.
  3. You DM me a screenshot of the rescue's acknowledgement of your application, plus the key phrase at the end of these terms.
  4. On 2 November, I draw 4 winners at random. Each gets a foster starter kit delivered to their door.
  5. Bonus: once a foster cat is placed with you, send me a photo and you'll get food for your foster cat too.
  6. Winners are featured on my account by first name, rescue and city (no faces), with updates on their foster cat.

Full terms

01

Who runs this

This giveaway is run by me personally, as the owner of the account that posted the "Can I save 5 cats before I leave Australia?" video, with a friend contributing the food bonus. It is not run, sponsored or endorsed by any animal rescue, Kmart, Royal Canin, Instagram, Meta or TikTok. By taking part you release Instagram and TikTok from any responsibility for it.

02

Who can enter

To enter you must:

  • be 18 or over and live in Australia, with an Australian delivery address;
  • never have been a foster carer for any animal, with any rescue, shelter or council, before this giveaway;
  • submit an application to foster a cat with a registered animal rescue organisation in Australia (for example, one listed on PetRescue) between 23 October and 1 November 2026, and receive an acknowledgement of that application from the rescue.

One entry per person and one per household.

03

How to enter

Send me a direct message on the account where you saw the video, with:

  • a screenshot of the rescue's acknowledgement of your application (an email or message) that shows the rescue's name and the date. You can blur anything else that's personal;
  • a line confirming you've never fostered before;
  • the key phrase, word for word. It's at the end of these terms.

DMs without the key phrase won't be counted. I'll reply to confirm your entry. I may ask a follow-up question, or check with the rescue, to confirm your application.

04

Dates and the draw

  • Entries open on 23 October 2026 and close at 11:59pm AEDT on Sunday 1 November 2026.
  • On Monday 2 November 2026, I'll draw 4 winners at random from all valid entries using an online random picker, and record the draw so it can be shared.
  • I'll DM each winner and announce them on my account. Winners have 48 hours to reply with a delivery address. If a winner doesn't reply in time, I'll draw a replacement.
05

What winners get

A foster starter kit, ordered by me from Kmart Australia and delivered to your address. Each kit includes:

  • a cat carrier;
  • a litter box and scoop;
  • cat litter;
  • food and water bowls;
  • a cardboard scratcher;
  • a blanket;
  • kitten-safe toys.

If you already have some of these, tell me and I'll try to swap them for something else you need. The kit is supplies only. It can't be exchanged for cash or gift cards, and it can't be transferred. If an item is out of stock, I'll swap it for something similar. Delivery is handled by Kmart, so delivery delays, damage in transit and product faults go through Kmart's normal returns process.

06

Bonus: food for your foster cat

Once a foster cat has been placed with you, DM me a photo of your foster cat. My Sydney-based friend Bess will then send you cat food (dry and wet, for example Royal Canin, chosen to suit your cat's age) or a voucher to buy food or supplies. The bonus is open to the 4 winners, for a placement made by 30 November 2026.

07

Your fostering

Your foster arrangement is between you and your rescue. I'm not involved in approvals, matching cats, vet care or anything else about your placement. Winners are expected to follow through with their application in good faith. If fostering doesn't work out, please pass the supplies on to your rescue so they still help a cat.

08

Your details

I'll only use the screenshots you send to check your entry, and I'll delete entrants' messages after the draw (or after delivery, for winners). I'll only use winners' full name, address and phone number to place the delivery, which means sharing them with Kmart and, for the food bonus, with my friend. I won't share them with anyone else or post them.

09

Featuring winners

So everyone can see the kits went to real foster carers, featuring winners is part of the giveaway. By entering, you agree that if you win, I can share publicly on my Instagram and TikTok (in posts, reels or stories):

  • your first name;
  • the rescue you applied to;
  • your city or town, and state.

I won't show your face unless you ask me to. After the draw, I'll check in now and then to ask for photos or stories about your foster cat to share. If you'd like something taken down later, DM me and I'll remove it.

10

Fair play

I can refuse an entry that doesn't meet these terms, or that looks false or misleading, including after the draw. If something outside my control comes up, I may change or end the giveaway, and I'll say so publicly on the account. My decisions about entries and the draw are final.

11

The key phrase

If you've read this far, thank you. Put this exact sentence in your entry DM to show you've read and agree to these terms:

"I agree to the T&Cs, and Tictac deserves bum pats."
